# Summarising

1. **Ask yourself who it is for**: by default the owner, who wants to decide whether this matters and what to do.
2. **Shape**: one-sentence bottom line first. Then 3-6 bullets with the key facts, numbers, decisions or
   arguments, in order of importance, not in the order the source used. Then "What it means for you" or
   "Next steps" only if there is something to act on.
3. **Faithful, not creative**: no facts that are not in the source. Keep numbers, names and dates exact. Mark
   opinions as the author's ("the author argues…").
4. **Compress hard**: a summary should be under a fifth of the original. Drop examples, history and
   repetition unless they are the point.
5. **Web pages**: `read_webpage` once, ignore navigation/cookie text in the output, and link the source.
6. **Long documents the owner pasted**: if it was cut off, say which part you summarised.
7. **Meetings/conversations**: decisions, owners of action items with dates, open questions.
